the rogue astronaut

I'm Sabastian — a Biomedical Engineering graduate who operates at the intersection of precision and rebellion. I balance three worlds: clinical engineering (keeping patients alive, one device at a time), 3D design (building in SolidWorks, ANSYS, and Blender), and visual storytelling (crafting narratives through photo and video editing). I don't just cross disciplines — I weld them together.

⚕ clinical engineering in action

On the clinical side, I've been in the trenches — from operating rooms at UGMC to rural diagnostic centres across Ghana. I've installed over 50 medical devices, from haematology analysers to ICU ventilators, and trained clinicians to trust the technology in their hands.

I've stood by during surgeries, supporting Olympus LASER and ERBE ESU systems while a patient's life hung in the balance. 30% reduction in equipment downtime. 95%+ uptime across multiple sites. IEC 60601 and ISO 13485 compliance — because that's patient safety.
